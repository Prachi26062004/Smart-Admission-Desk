FAQ

Common questions

Do I still need to visit the office?

Only for original document verification, after your online upload is accepted.

What if my document is rejected?

You see the reason straight away and can upload a corrected copy.

How do I follow my renewal?

The Requests tab shows a progress bar from submitted to ready.

Is my data safe?

Accounts are password protected, and only you and admission staff see your records.
